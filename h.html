HERE THE SHORT STORY OF SPIDERMAN

Spider-Man, originally Peter Parker, is a teenage orphan who gains superhuman strength, speed, agility, and wall-crawling abilities after being bitten by a radioactive spider. Initially using his new powers for personal gain as a television wrestler, he refuses to stop a burglar who later murders his beloved Uncle Ben.
Realizing that "with great power comes great responsibility," Peter dedicates his life to fighting crime in New York City as the friendly neighborhood superhero Spider-Man.
